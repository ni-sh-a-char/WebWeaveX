## Maintainer Responsibilities

- Review and merge pull requests
- Triage issues
- Make release decisions
- Approve architectural changes
- Maintain security policy
- Respond to security reports within 48 hours

## Becoming a Maintainer

Contributors who have made significant, sustained contributions may be nominated as maintainers. Requirements:

1. 10+ merged pull requests
2. Demonstrated understanding of the codebase
3. Active for 3+ months
4. Nomination by an existing maintainer
